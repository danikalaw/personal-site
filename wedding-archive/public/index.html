Dress Code

We're going for a dressy-casual summer wedding. The festivities will be outdoors in the August sun, so don't wear something that'll make you sweat. However, you might want to bring a light jacket or sweater so you can layer up when the sun goes down.

  • Wear a nice dress shirt or polo and slacks
  • Wear a light summer dress
  • Ties and bow ties are optional, but cool
  • Jackets and vests are optional (but nice to have when it gets cool in the evening)
  • Don't wear shorts
  • Don't wear a t-shirt
  • Don't wear pointy heels (they might get stuck in the lawn!)
  • Don't wear something from the closet of Marie Antoinette
  • Don't wear He-Man cosplay
